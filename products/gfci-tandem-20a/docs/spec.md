# GFCI Tandem 20A Product Specification

## Form Factor
- Siemens QT-style tandem footprint (compatible with existing 2×15A AFCI tandem)

## Electrical Specifications
- Two independent 20A 120V circuits
- Class A GFCI protection (5 mA trip threshold)

## Features
- Each circuit has dedicated HOT and NEUTRAL terminals
- Each circuit has independent TEST/RESET buttons
- Circuits trip independently

## Certification
- Designed to be certifiable to UL 489 (Molded-Case Circuit Breakers) and UL 943 (Ground-Fault Circuit-Interrupters)
- Manufacturing and certification outsourced
