(a) When you access or use the Services, information may be provided directly by you. This can include your name, email address, organization details, account credentials, and any other information you choose to enter into forms, fields, or configuration tools within our web applications.

3 Information Collected or Generated Automatically

(a) The Services may operate in a manner that results in the automatic collection or generation of technical and usage-related data when you visit our websites or use our applications. This can include information about your browser, device, and network environment, such as browser type and version, operating system, user agent, screen and viewport dimensions, time zone, language settings, hardware characteristics exposed by the browser, connection type, Internet Protocol (IP) address, and other similar attributes.

(b) As you interact with the Services, logs and records of your activity may be created. These may reflect, among other things, times of access, pages or views visited, features used, and application events associated with your actions. In the case of interactive or design-oriented applications, such events may indicate, by way of example and without limitation, that a session has begun or ended, that a design or configuration has loaded or been saved, that particular elements have been added, moved, resized, renamed, recolored, removed, selected, deselected, or otherwise manipulated, that configuration parameters such as counts or dimensions have been altered, that certain actions could not be completed or were blocked for defined reasons, and that options such as reset, cancel, share, or copy were invoked.

(c) The Services may record diagnostic and performance-related information, such as error messages, crash reports, response times, and other operational metrics associated with our systems. This type of information can be useful in understanding how the Services perform and in identifying areas where behavior differs from expected patterns.

(a) Some of our applications or supporting tools may make use of identifiers derived from technical characteristics of your browser or device, sometimes referred to as device fingerprints or similar terminology. These identifiers can be derived from combinations of information elements that your environment exposes, such as graphics or canvas rendering behavior, WebGL capabilities, audio processing behavior, font availability or rendering characteristics, screen configuration, supported features, time zone and locale, and other browser- or device-level properties.

(b) These identifiers are generally used to distinguish or associate sessions, configurations, or interactions with particular browsers or devices for purposes that can include understanding how the Services are used, limiting abuse, maintaining the integrity of stored configurations, or supporting security-related analysis. The particular signals used and the methods by which an identifier is derived can vary over time and may be adjusted as our technical approach evolves.

(c) The Services may also rely on technologies such as cookies, local storage, and other browser-based mechanisms. These mechanisms may be used to maintain session state, remember preferences, associate your browser with stored configurations or designs, and facilitate logging or batching of application events and analytics information. Depending on your browser settings, certain of these mechanisms may be subject to controls that you can exercise, and if you choose to block or delete some of them, portions of the Services may not function as intended.
